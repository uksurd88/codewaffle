I'm a bioinformatician (PhD, Max Planck Society, *magna cum laude*) and program leader based in Den Bosch. My work sits where biology, data, and AI collide: leading cross-functional teams that turn messy, large-scale biomedical data into decisions and products.

Today I'm Project Lead at [ENPICOM](https://www.enpicom.com), advancing AI-based antibody and biologics discovery on an ISO 27001 and FAIR-compliant platform. Before that I spent close to two years consulting worldwide through RadTech and Assal Energy on AI and blockchain for drug discovery, renewables, DeSci, and government programs across the UAE, China, India, LATAM, and South Africa.

Earlier chapters include leading AI biomarker discovery at Genialis, advising Top 15 pharma on FAIR and knowledge engineering at The Hyve, and a PhD on H3K4 methyltransferases in mouse embryonic stem cells at Max Planck with six peer-reviewed papers. I also completed Wharton's Entrepreneurship specialization, the McKinsey Forward Program, and IBM's Generative AI for Executives.

I enjoy being the translator between lab scientists, engineers, and C-suite. I'm happiest when a complex data problem turns into something a bench scientist, or an agent, can actually use.

Outside work I DJ, hike, play ultimate frisbee, and have volunteered on trail maintenance teams in Norway and Iceland. Feel free to get in touch via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vanbug) or [GitHub](https://github.com/uksurd88).
